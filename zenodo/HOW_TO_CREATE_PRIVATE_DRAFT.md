# Zenodo draft (do not publish yet)

1. Sign in at https://zenodo.org/ and select **New upload**.
2. Upload `IFAC_Zenodo_software_data_DRAFT_2026-11-03.zip` (prepared separately).
3. Set resource type **Software** and copy the title, creators and abstract from `METADATA_DRAFT.json`, correcting with both authors.
4. Choose a license only after both authors have agreed. Enter no invented funding, grant, ORCID or DOI.
5. Select **Save draft**, not **Publish**. Drafts are unpublished.
6. Use **Share** on the draft to invite Alexander (Can edit or Can preview draft).
7. After acceptance of the manuscript and coauthor approval, review privacy/supplementary requirements, decide whether to publish data/code and assign a DOI.

Zenodo metadata become publicly accessible for *published* records even when files are restricted. Do not confuse a restricted published record with an unpublished private draft.
