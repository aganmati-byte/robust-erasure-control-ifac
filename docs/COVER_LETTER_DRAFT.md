Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Robust Mean-Square Stabilization of an Uncertain Scalar Pole under Bernoulli Erasures: Controller-Class Limits, Certified Periodic Policies, and Quadratic Performance,”** for publication as a research article in *IFAC Journal of Systems and Control*.

We study the mean-square stabilization of a scalar unstable plant with a fixed but unknown parameter in a prescribed interval when full-state observations arrive intermittently through an independent Bernoulli erasure channel. We characterize feasibility within a specifically restricted stationary last-reception linear-feedback class, distinguish these limits from what periodic and additional-measurement policies can achieve, and provide independently checkable polynomial/rational stability certificates. Exact physical-time quadratic moments allow matched performance comparisons between a feasibility-oriented stationary design and a cost-aware alternative. The numerical study includes reproducible deterministic and paired stochastic evaluations and explicitly separates continuum certificates from finite-grid and finite-horizon evidence.

We have situated the question against classical works on stochastic packet loss, robust simultaneous stabilization and periodic control, including Elia, Tannenbaum, Khargonekar–Poolla–Tannenbaum, and Okano–Ishii. Our claims do **not** extend the stationary-class threshold to arbitrary causal controllers. We provide reproducibility scripts, mathematical verification material and limitations.
