**Scope searched:** openly indexed publisher pages, arXiv, bibliographic discovery, and accessible scholarly search results through 20 October 2026. The exact Scopus and Web of Science subscription indexes, citation network completeness, and all paywalled full texts were **not accessible**. SciSpace semantic search was additionally attempted but retrieved numerous irrelevant matches. This is not a claim of a comprehensive Scopus, WoS or Google Scholar census.

**Target model:** one scalar fixed-but-unknown `a ∈ [a1,a2]`, plant `x[k+1]=a*x[k]+u[k]+w[k]`, i.i.d. Bernoulli sensor-state reception `q`, full precise state on a successful transmission, causal actions and an age-indexed gain sequence `u[k]=-h[k-tau]*x[tau]`. The gain table is fixed and does not depend on plant `a`. `q` may be known or unknown as specified. Mean-square stability means bounded physical-time second moment under nondegenerate independent noise. Strict contraction, not just bounded finite-horizon Monte Carlo, establishes stability.

## Verdict for each proposed manuscript contribution

1. `q_stat^alg=max{1-1/a2², ((a1+a2)²-4)/(4a1a2)}`: **mathematically supported for the specified stationary last-reception class, PRIORITY UNRESOLVED**. Cannot assert new theorem merely because exact display equation wasn't found.
2. Hardy-space endpoint converse and gain realization: **credible specialization of classical interpolation and H² norm geometry**. Could be a nontrivial synthesis, but independently establish whether it is implied by Elia 2005 / Tannenbaum 1980 / Cui–Lindquist 2024.
3. Higher-coverage information structures outperform stationarity: **general phenomenon known**; the exact constructed memory-three endpoint certificate is a concrete worked case only, not a full-interval theorem.
4. Ordinary-Chebyshev exact family threshold: **class-specific identity from classical polynomial minimax**, no claim of new general periodic optimality.
5. Optimized weighted period-six continuum certificates: **reproducible constructive computational comparisons**, not a mathematical novelty theorem or proof of period-six global optimality.
6. Exact physical-time moments and cost-aware stationary design: **independently derived and validated useful analysis**, but renewal moment techniques and cost optimization are classical; possible value is as a restricted explanatory benchmark, not proven landmark novelty.
7. Rate-universality and inherited separate learning claims: **not claimed**; archived pending separate verification.

**Evidence status:** distinct assumptions found; no explicit same theorem identified; this does NOT establish originality. Priority question is still decisive and must be checked by an independent control theorist, including citation-chaining unavailable here.
